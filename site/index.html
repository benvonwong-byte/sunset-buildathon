Sunset Groups

How this works

Every morning a script reads the public calendars of the groups listed here, the San Francisco Public Library, SF Rec & Park, and the Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup and Funcheap listings for this part of the city. It keeps what falls inside the Sunset and merges anything listed twice. Times are Pacific. Check the organizer's page before you head out.

Missing a group? . The code is open for the buildathon: github.com/benvonwong-byte/sunset-buildathon.
