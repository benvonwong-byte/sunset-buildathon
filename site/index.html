How this calendar works

A script reads the public calendars of the groups in the directory, the San Francisco Public Library, and the Luma, Eventbrite and Meetup listings for this part of the city, every morning. It keeps the events whose address falls inside the Sunset and merges the ones listed in more than one place. Times are Pacific. Dates and rooms change, so check the organizer's page before you head out.

The code is open, and a buildathon is welcome to it: github.com/benvonwong-byte/sunset-buildathon. If your group posts events somewhere we can read, .

Keys: 1 to 4 switch views, / searches, j and k move through events, Enter opens one, Esc closes.
