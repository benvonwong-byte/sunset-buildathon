Sunset Events

Public events between Lincoln Way and Sloat, Stanyan and the ocean, gathered every morning from , the library, Luma, Eventbrite and Meetup.

How this works

A script reads the public calendars of the groups on our map, the San Francisco Public Library, SF Rec & Park, and the Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup and Funcheap listings for this part of the city, every morning. It keeps what falls inside the Sunset and merges anything listed twice. Times are Pacific. Dates and rooms change, so check the organizer's page before you head out.

The code is open for the buildathon: github.com/benvonwong-byte/sunset-buildathon. If your group posts events somewhere we can read, .

Keys: / searches, j and k move through events, Enter opens one, g switches to groups.
